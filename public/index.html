What the apps cover

  • Australia and Singapore in full, five main sectors each. Australia: superannuation, banking, insurance, health and critical infrastructure. Singapore: banking, insurance, capital markets, listed companies and critical infrastructure. Each main sector has its own starting positions and worked examples; other sectors use a best-practice baseline or the general starting positions.
  • Best-practice baselines for any jurisdiction. Eight sector profiles drawn from international standard setters, not one country's rules: banking; insurance; securities and investment advice; health care; payments and money transmission; critical infrastructure, run by government or by a company; and government in general. A baseline names no regulator. Your own obligations come from the matter brief.
  • Public bodies. For government and government-run infrastructure, the apps name the accountable authority where they would otherwise say board, on screen and in every download.
  • Four controls in every baseline. Controls on any AI use that decides or acts (what it may touch, where its actions are logged, who checks before it acts, how it is stopped); a fast path the board sets for low-risk uses; one record of each provider's evidence, used for every regulator; and two measures that show whether governance speeds AI use or slows it.
  • Another jurisdiction. The apps are open source under the Apache License 2.0, and the method for adding a jurisdiction is published with them.
